TRADING PSYCHOLOGY TRAINER - PWA DEPLOY NOTES
=============================================
Upload this WHOLE folder (keep the structure) to any HTTPS host, e.g. GitHub Pages / Netlify:

    index.html
    sw.js
    manifest.webmanifest
    icons/  (6 PNG files)

Then open the https:// address on your phone:
  Android / Chrome : an "Install app" button appears at the top -> tap it.
  iPhone / Safari  : tap Share -> "Add to Home Screen" (a hint appears in the app).

WHEN YOU CHANGE index.html LATER
  1. Open sw.js and change   const VERSION = "tpt-v1";   to   "tpt-v2"  (then v3 ...).
  2. Upload index.html and sw.js.
  Users see "A new version is ready - Update"; their saved career resumes automatically.

NOTES
  - Works offline after the first visit.
  - Opening index.html by double-click (file://) still works as a normal web page; install/offline need https.
  - Saved careers live in the browser's localStorage, not in the app cache, so updates never delete them.
  - After deploying, run Lighthouse (Chrome DevTools > Lighthouse > PWA) once to confirm.
